The documentation describes how Agentic SWE works today. This blog is where we explain why it works that way, and what changed between releases.

## What to expect

- **Release notes** that go past the changelog: what a change means for a work item, a gate, or a host.
- **Design decisions**, such as why approval always waits for a person and why work state lives in files in your repository.
- **Field notes** from running the pipeline on real changes, including the parts that did not work.

## What stays the same

The pack is MIT licensed and runs locally. There is no hosted runtime, and nothing on this blog changes that. If you are evaluating the project, [the evaluation page](/evaluate) is still the fastest place to start, and [the golden path](/docs/golden-path) gets a first work item through a gate in about fifteen minutes.
